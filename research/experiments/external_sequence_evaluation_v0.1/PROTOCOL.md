# External sequence evaluation protocol

Fixed on 19 November 2026 before reading the selected corpus texts.

## Question and selection

Do technique-only traversals and occurrence-preserving joins give different three-technique answers on explicit source-described sequences from additional publishers? Select all five rank-three reports from the frozen AnnoCTR donor frame, with no substitution. The existing seed and frame hashes determine the selection. This is a purposive five-donor extension, not a random sample of all threat reporting. Previous report groups and the two DFIR cases stay separate.

## Interpretation contract

Read each complete corpus text. Retain distinct actions and explicit temporal or execution-order statements only. Do not infer BEFORE from prose order, generic capability lists, causal association alone, or different campaigns. Keep separate connected components for different described episodes. No transitive closure. Record source offsets and hashes for actions, edges and consequential exclusions; raw source texts are not distributed.

Distinguish incident observations from described execution procedures or conditional chains. Only incident-linked sequences enter the primary stratum. Procedure sequences may enter a separately named secondary stratum; they never establish real-world event occurrence or incident prevalence. Reports without a qualifying sequence remain in the selected-report denominator; their graph-query error counts are unassessed, not zero.

Use parent-level Enterprise ATT&CK v12.1 mappings. Each mapping needs a stated rationale. Ambiguous mappings remain empty sets. Record excluded candidate chains and the strongest alternative reading. Interpretation is exploratory and not independently validated.

## Fixed query and comparison rule

For every retained graph, enumerate all ordered triples over its declared candidate parent-technique vocabulary, allowing repetition. Query selection does not depend on whether a model makes an error. Compare ordinary distinct-pair traversal, loop-preserving traversal, distinct-pair endpoint joins and loop-preserving endpoint joins against direct enumeration of three distinct occurrence paths. All four receive identical mappings and explicit edges. They are representation controls, not executions of published extraction systems.

Report per graph and per stratum: selected and usable reports, retained actions and edges, source paths, fully mapped paths, query-universe size, supported queries, unknown-mapping-dependent queries, decidable unsupported queries, returned answers, unsupported positives on decidable queries, missed supported queries and unresolved-query returns. Never score an unresolved query as a negative. Unknown sets may receive any queried label; fixed known sets and edges are not revised by this uncertainty model.

Report query-specific uncertainty separately from Boolean graph outputs. Include all-correct/no-advantage cases. Do not pool overlapping sensitivity profiles as independent observations, use query counts as sample size, infer prevalence, or claim extraction accuracy or analyst time savings. Include source evidence completeness and interpretation limitations alongside numeric results.

## Reproduction and completion

Freeze selection and this protocol in main before content review. Publish source-bound interpretations, exclusions, a deterministic evaluator, regression checks and machine-readable results. Recheck local source bytes against the original frame hashes, allowing CRLF recovery only when it reproduces those hashes exactly. Audit result regeneration in CI. A fresh checkout must reproduce public-record results without private source files; semantic source rechecking requires the separately obtained corpus.

Browser visual/accessibility review and practical analyst efficacy are separate work items. This comparison alone cannot establish either.
